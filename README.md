# 🐕 DOGE MINER 🐕

A fun clicker/idle game where you mine Doges and become rich! Built with vanilla JavaScript, HTML5, and CSS3.

## 🎮 Play Now

**Best way to play on Chromebook:**

1. Go to: https://gamerfornite049-lang.github.io/Doge-clicker/
2. Click the doge and start mining!

*(Or open `index.html` directly if you downloaded the repo)*

## 🎯 How to Play

### Click to Mine
- **Click the Doge** to earn Doges instantly
- Each click gives you coins based on your current click power

### Buy Upgrades
- Increase your **click power** with upgrades
- Each upgrade makes your clicks worth more
- Costs scale up exponentially as you buy more

### Hire Miners
- **Buildings** (Puppy Miners, Doge Drills, etc.) generate passive income
- They work even while you're not clicking
- Each building type gets more expensive as you buy more

### Progress & Goals
- Watch your Doge count grow
- Buy better upgrades and buildings
- Unlock more powerful miners
- Try to reach higher milestones

## 💾 Features

✅ **Auto-Save** - Your progress saves automatically every click  
✅ **Manual Save/Load** - Save and load your game with buttons  
✅ **Reset** - Start over if you want  
✅ **Responsive Design** - Works on mobile, tablet, and desktop  
✅ **No Ads or Login** - Pure gameplay  
✅ **Offline-Friendly** - Works without internet once loaded
